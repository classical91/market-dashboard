Send briefings from the iPhone Shortcut
  1. After the Ask ChatGPT step, add Get Contents of URL.
  2. URL: /api/phone-briefings/geopolitics
  3. Method POST. Header x-broadcast-key = your BROADCAST_LEDGER_API_KEY.
  4. Request Body JSON, one field: text = the ChatGPT result.
  5. Repeat for each desk, changing the last part of the URL to geopolitics, economics, stocks or crypto.

Sending the same text twice is harmless: it is stored once.
