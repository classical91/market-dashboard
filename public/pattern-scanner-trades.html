The pairs you've starred, each with its directional bias, local extremes and pattern status side by side — context on what you're following, not a recommendation
ℹ️ How this page works
  1. Star a setup in Pattern Scanner, or add it here. This page collects those saved symbol/timeframe pairs and reports the full state of each one. For trades that didn't come from a screener, search the top 30 tokens (or type any Binance USDT pair) above the cards and press Track.
  2. Read the three engines separately. Directional bias is the environment, local extremes are where price sits inside it, and the pattern is the structure forming — they measure different things and are never merged.
  3. Use the context strip for agreement, not instruction. ALIGNED, MIXED, CONFLICT, QUIET and PARTIAL describe whether the engines currently point the same way — PARTIAL means an engine is down and there is too little left to compare. Deciding what to do with any of it is yours.
  4. Hand a card to Analysis Trader (GPT). The 🤖 button at the foot of each card copies that card's bias, extremes, patterns, OI, RSI, evidence and data age as text, then opens the Analysis Trader custom GPT with it prefilled. If ChatGPT opens with an empty box, just paste — it's already on your clipboard. CoinGlass Super Chart opens that token's Binance perp chart on CoinGlass.
  5. Check the DATA line before you trust the card. It reports how long ago the newest closed candle closed and how long ago each engine last recalculated. A card flagged STALE is showing evidence the feed or the cache has stopped advancing — the numbers are real, but they are not now.
  6. Unstar to remove it. This is a research watchlist; it does not place, manage, or close trades.
  • Candle age is not the same as staleness. On a 4h card the newest closed candle is normally anything up to four hours old — that is the timeframe, not a fault. STALE appears only once the age runs past a whole bar plus half a bar of slack, which means the feed is behind rather than simply mid-bar.
  • No combined score. Averaging direction, exhaustion and structure into one number would hide exactly the disagreement worth seeing — a BULLISH bias beside a confirmed top is information, not an error.
  • Open interest and multi-timeframe RSI sit beside the three engines, not inside the context strip. OI says whether perp positions are opening or closing on the horizon matching the card (daily and weekly cards use 24H); RSI shows RSI 14 on each timeframe enabled on the RSI Matrix. Neither changes ALIGNED, MIXED or CONFLICT.
  • AI use: No. Every number here comes from the same fixed rules Directional Bias, Local Extremes, Pattern Scanner, Crypto Open Interest and the RSI Matrix already run; this page only assembles them.
