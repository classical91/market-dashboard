Send screenshots from your iPhone (Share Sheet Shortcut)

Opening this page on your phone and tapping + Add screenshots works too. The Shortcut lets you send straight from Photos or a screenshot preview with Share → Potential Buys.

  1. In Shortcuts, make a new shortcut named Potential Buys. In its details, turn on Show in Share Sheet and set it to receive Images.
  2. Add Repeat with Each item in Shortcut Input (so several screenshots go at once).
  3. Inside the repeat, add Convert Image → JPEG (camera photos are HEIC, which browsers can't show).
  4. Then Get Contents of URL:
    • URL: /api/potential-buys/upload
    • Method POST. Header x-broadcast-key = your BROADCAST_LEDGER_API_KEY (the same key as the phone briefings Shortcut).
    • Request Body File = Converted Image.
  5. Optional: add Ask for Input before the URL step and put it at the end of the URL as ?label= + the answer, to label it with a ticker.

The key can only add screenshots here. Viewing, relabelling and deleting them still needs your login.
