📡 Signal Diagnostics
Signal plumbing and archived ledgers — what the Decision Engine, Live Scanner and Signal Bot are doing, and the retired paper books. None of it feeds a demo account.
These panels moved out of the Backtest Lab because none of them serve a demo account. They are observation surfaces: signal sources, loop health, and read-only history.
Nothing here can open a position or write to a strategy ledger. The Decision Engine's plans are scored in an unattributed, non-persistent context; the Signal Bot has no registered strategy identity at all.

Live Scanner
Native Bitget perpetual scanner — pulls its own candles and runs its own strategy on a 60s loop, so no TradingView webhook is needed. Evaluates closed candles only, one entry per symbol/direction/candle, paper trades only. Enable with ENABLE_LIVE_SCANNER=true.
