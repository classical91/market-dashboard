ℹ️ How this page works
  1. One shared feed. The server holds one KuCoin public WebSocket per market (wss://x-push-spot.kucoin.com, wss://x-push-futures.kucoin.com) and subscribes to the trade channel for the pairs being watched. Browsers read the server over Server-Sent Events; no browser connects to KuCoin and no API key is used.
  2. Symbols are resolved from KuCoin. BTC, ETH and SOL map to the listed USDT spot pair and USDT-margined perpetual (bitcoin futures are XBTUSDTM) from KuCoin’s instrument metadata.
  3. Notional. Spot: price × quantity. Futures: price × contracts × the contract multiplier from KuCoin’s metadata. Futures quantity is shown in contracts, never as coins.
  4. Side is the taker side. KuCoin documents a trade’s side as the taker order’s side, so “buy” is an aggressive buy. Delta = aggressive buy notional − aggressive sell notional; buy pressure = buy notional ÷ classified notional.
  5. CVD is the running sum of signed notional (USDT, or base units on request) since the session shown under the chart. It restarts when you change market or pair, or press Reset.
  • One venue only. Volume, delta and CVD describe KuCoin’s public trades for one instrument, not the whole market or other exchanges.
  • Partial windows are labelled. A rolling window is complete only after the server has been subscribed for the whole window without a reconnect.
  • Read-only. Nothing on this page can place orders or access an account. AI use: No.
