⚖️ Local Extremes
Whether price is stretched near a possible local top or bottom across the shared 25-token universe — location, not direction

Local Extremes measures whether price appears stretched near a possible local top or bottom. A confirmed extreme can occur inside a larger trend.

Bottom and top are scored independently from Bollinger excursion, RSI extreme, regular divergence, liquidity sweep, volume climax and reversal confirmation. Both scores are shown, always: Bottom 45 / Top 75 is the reading, and the weaker side building is information too.

A bullish trend and a confirmed local top can both be true.

Directional Bias = direction. Local Extremes = location within the move. A top or bottom here is market context, never an automatic entry — nothing on this page means BUY, SELL, LONG or SHORT.

ℹ️ How this page works
  1. Choose a timeframe and rescan. Rows are scored on closed candles from the shared token universe, and the same cached scan feeds Directional Bias — the two pages always describe the same candles.
  2. Read both scores, not just the leader. Bottom and top stay independent; the Extreme column only names which side currently leads.
  3. Separate candidates from confirmation. A candidate has exhaustion evidence; confirming and confirmed states also require a Bollinger reclaim or a micro-structure break. Rows rank CONFIRMED, CONFIRMING, CANDIDATE, WATCH, NONE, with the leading score breaking ties.
  4. Check freshness before trusting a row. FRESH, STALE and UNAVAILABLE come from the shared freshness rules; STALE means the feed or the cache has stopped advancing.
  • An extreme is a location, not an entry. TOP CONFIRMED says price is stretched and has begun to turn, not “sell now”. Setups with an entry, stop and target belong to the Decision Engine.
  • Exhaustion is not direction. A high bottom score can coexist with a strong BEARISH bias, and a high top score with a BULLISH one — all four combinations are legitimate. Use the disagreement as a warning not to chase price into a stretched move; My Trades reads both together.
  • AI use: No. Every score comes from fixed, inspectable indicator rules; this page does not call an AI model.
  • Probability, not certainty. Even a 100 score means all configured evidence is present, not that price must reverse.
