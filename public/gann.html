ℹ️ How this page works
  1. Pick an asset and timeframe. Candles are the same cached Binance spot klines the screeners use; only closed candles are analysed, so every number changes only when a new candle closes.
  2. Choose the anchor. Auto picks the most extreme confirmed swing in the last 150 bars (a swing is confirmed only after 5 bars on each side). Manual lets you click any candle; its high or low becomes the anchor.
  3. Set the price-to-time scale. The 1x1 angle moves one scale unit per bar: ATR(14) by default, range / bars, or a value you type. All lines are computed in price-and-bar units, so zoom and resize never move them.
  4. Read confluence as a count. The matrix groups levels from different methods within ¼ ATR. A cluster's number is how many independent methods agree — not a probability.
  5. Check the backtest. Gann levels are compared with control levels of the same density, cycle hits with the hit rate of random timing, and filtered strategies with the unfiltered one, after costs.
  • AI use: No. Every level comes from fixed, documented formulas (docs/gann-intelligence.md); this page does not call an AI model.
  • Not a signal. Nothing here means BUY or SELL. TraderClaw receives the same observations as read-only, experimental context.
  • No fabricated data. When candles are unavailable the page says so rather than drawing anything.
