ℹ️ How this page works
  1. Source. The CFTC’s weekly Commitments of Traders report (Legacy, futures only) from publicreporting.cftc.gov. It’s free and public domain. Positions are as of Tuesday and published Friday at 3:30pm ET. The server fetches it once an hour at most and keeps the last good report if the CFTC is unreachable.
  2. Open interest plots the % change in total open interest over the lookback (1 or 4 weekly reports). Contract counts are never plotted directly: a Bitcoin contract and a Natural Gas contract share no unit.
  3. COT positioning plots net non-commercial (large speculator) longs minus shorts as a % of open interest. That’s the direction of the speculative crowd, a different quantity from OI. Every row is labelled with the metric it shows.
  4. The ellipse. Rings are value bands from the inner ring (−scale) through the dashed ring (0) to the outer ring (+scale). The geometry follows the reference Pine script’s sqrt(1 − x²) half-ellipse, mirrored.
  5. ROLL marks quarterly contracts within three weeks of a March/June/September/December expiry. Large OI swings there are usually contracts rolling, not new positioning.
  • Daily is unavailable. No free, licensed daily open-interest feed exists for these exchanges.
  • Not LuxAlgo’s formula. The reference indicator’s calculation isn’t public, so this page doesn’t claim to reproduce its numbers.
  • Missing is missing. A market absent from the report shows — and leaves a gap in the shape. It is never plotted as 0.
