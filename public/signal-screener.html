ℹ️ How this page works
  1. Choose a timeframe and rescan. Both engines evaluate the same closed candles, shared token universe, and cache.
  2. Start with Local extremes for exhaustion. Bottom and top scores remain separate and combine Bollinger stretch, RSI extreme, regular divergence, liquidity sweep, volume climax, and reversal confirmation.
  3. Switch to Directional bias for the environment. BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL plus a score measuring agreement across RSI, MACD, VWAP, volume, EMA trend, and EMA200.
  4. Separate candidates from confirmation. A candidate has exhaustion evidence; confirming and confirmed states also require a Bollinger reclaim or micro-structure break.
  • Bias is not an entry. BULLISH 67 means four of six checks lean bullish, not “go long now”. This page reports two things — bias (direction of the environment) and extreme (where price sits within it). LONG / SHORT stay reserved for pages that publish an actual setup with an entry, stop, and target, such as the Decision Engine.
  • AI use: No. Both scores come from fixed, inspectable indicator rules; this page does not call an AI model.
  • Probability, not certainty. Even a 100 score means all configured evidence is present, not that price must reverse.
