ℹ️ How this page works
  1. Scanner detections are logged. New breakouts and divergences enter the tracker with their direction, price, and detection time.
  2. Price resolves each result. The window starts at the close of the detecting candle (24h for 1h, 4 days for 4h, 10 days for 1D). The outcome uses the 1h candle close at the end of that window, not the price when the tracker next runs, and the same candles give the best (MFE) and worst (MAE) move along the way. A move of 2% or more in the called direction is a hit; 2% or more against it is a miss; anything smaller is flat.
  3. Judge the sample, not one call. Use the overall and per-pattern totals to see where the scanner has evidence and where the sample is still thin.
  • AI use: No. Hit rates come from logged scanner detections and subsequent price outcomes, not model-generated analysis.
