📊 RSI Matrix
How strong or weak each market is right now, on four timeframes at once — RSI 14 on close, closed bars only

Each cell is RSI 14 for one market on one timeframe. The AVG row is the mean of every enabled timeframe.

Cell colours describe momentum: green from 55 up, red below 45, grey in between. The AVG row reads differently — an average at or above 70 is flagged MTF overbought and at or below 30 MTF oversold: stretched on every timeframe at once. These are RSI conditions, not BUY or SELL calls.

ℹ️ How this page works
  1. One RSI per cell. Every instrument × timeframe is fetched and computed on its own, so a failed GOLD 1H read leaves every other cell intact. RSI 14 uses the same Wilder formula as the other screeners, on closed bars only — a bar still forming never moves a value.
  2. AVG needs every timeframe. When one timeframe is unavailable the AVG shows — with how many readings exist (e.g. 3/4) instead of silently averaging fewer.
  3. Hover or tap a cell for the market, timeframe, exact value and state, or the reason a value is missing.
  4. Sorting is temporary. It reorders this view only; the configured order lives in Settings → Screeners → RSI Matrix, where instruments, sources and timeframes are edited.
  • Sources. Crypto pairs come from their exchange’s public candles (Binance, MEXC, KuCoin, Coinbase, Kraken, Poloniex). Indices, yields, futures and DXY come from Yahoo Finance chart history; 4H there is built from 1H bars on UTC boundaries. Stablecoin market caps come from CoinGecko.
  • Dominance and TOTAL series are sampled. USDT.D, USDC.D, BTC.D and TOTAL3 have no free historical feed, so the server records CoinGecko’s global figures every 15 minutes and builds bars from those observations. A timeframe shows “building history” until it has 15 consecutive bars — nothing older is invented.
  • Unavailable means unavailable. A missing value is shown as —, never as 0 and never replaced by another market.
  • AI use: No. Every value is a fixed RSI calculation; this page does not call an AI model.
