ℹ️ How this page works
  1. Four independent charts. Each panel has its own symbol, timeframe, refresh and fullscreen. Click the symbol to swap a panel (e.g. USDT.D → ETH); your symbols, timeframes and the sync setting are remembered in this browser. Reset layout restores TOTAL / DXY / BTC / USDT.D on 1H.
  2. Sync timeframes. When on, changing any panel’s timeframe moves all four to it. When off, every panel keeps its own.
  3. Header numbers. The value is the latest bar’s close; the percentage is its change against the previous bar’s close, the same convention as a TradingView legend. Hover it for the 24-hour change.
  4. Fullscreen. ⛶ expands one chart; ✕ or Esc returns to the matrix with its zoom and scroll intact.
  • Sources. The dot in each header names the feed; hover it for the exact provider symbol. Crypto pairs are Binance spot klines — the same BTCUSDT series Directional Bias and Local Extremes read. DXY is the ICE US Dollar Index (Yahoo Finance DX-Y.NYB), not a proxy; SPX, NDX, US10Y, gold and oil also come from Yahoo Finance, with 4H built from 1H bars on UTC boundaries.
  • TOTAL and dominance are sampled. No free feed serves the history of TOTAL, TOTAL2/3 or the .D series, so the server records CoinGecko’s global figures every 15 minutes and draws a line from those samples. The chart starts at the first sample, unsampled periods stay as gaps, and 5m is not offered. The ↗ link opens the same concept on TradingView for full history.
  • Unavailable means unavailable. If a feed fails, or a sampled series stops updating, the panel says Data unavailable with the reason and draws nothing — no stale or substitute values.
  • AI use: No. Every value is a market price or sampled figure; this page does not call an AI model and draws no bullish or bearish conclusions.
